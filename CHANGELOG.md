# Changelog

## FreeTV 2.0 · Glass Home 3.0 (November 2026)

- **7 new themes:** Plush, Limitless, Day & Night, Soft UI, Dot Matrix, Material and Minimalist. That's 13 in total.
- **Widgets on the TV's home screen:**
  - **11 widgets:** Clock, At a glance, Calendar, Weather, Sunrise & sunset, System, World clock, Countdown, Note, Quote of the day and Greeting.
  - **7 places** to put them, and **5 styles:** glass, no card, soft, material and dot matrix.
- **Widgets on the phone's home screen:** a TV remote, and quick actions (next look, screensaver, Home, free RAM).
- **New ways to style the home screen:**
  - **Background:** wallpaper, solid colour or gradient.
  - **Text:** light or dark.
  - **Tiles:** Glass, Plush, Soft or Material.
  - **More options:** a dot matrix clock, a names-only app list, and a shade along the bottom.
- **Install apps on the TV from your phone:**
  - Choose an APK, or Share one to FreeTV.
  - Split bundles (.apks / .xapk) are supported.
  - Shows progress, then lets you open the app or add it to the home screen.
- **Two new screensavers:** Dot matrix and Material.
- **FreeTV app:**
  - All themes now show at once in a grid, with a full-size page for each one.
  - New Widgets section with live samples.
  - New Style options.

## FreeTV 1.0.1 · Glass Home 2.5 (November 2026)

First public release.

- **FreeTV (phone):**
  - Liquid-glass design.
  - Setup wizard for any Android TV: finds the TV, installs Glass Home and picks a look.
  - Remote with a live picture of the TV.
  - Theme carousel and a designer for every detail.
  - Wallpapers and screensavers.
- **Glass Home (TV):**
  - 6 themes, 12 icon packs, 6 screensavers / watch faces.
  - Weather and sunrise/sunset.
  - Closes streaming apps when you press Home.
- **Install:** `FreeTV.apk` goes on your phone. `GlassHome.apk` is only needed if you want to install the TV app from a computer (`scripts\install-tv.bat`).
